1. Executive System Overview

The Barangay Zapatera E-Governance and Document Management System is an automated web and mobile platform engineered to modernize public governance, document issuance, resident management, and revenue auditing for Barangay Zapatera in Cebu City, Philippines. The system replaces manual paper transactions with an automated, tamper-resistant digital workflow governed by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). In order to maintain institutional accountability, data privacy compliance under Republic Act 10173, and financial integrity, the platform partitions system capabilities across five distinct user roles: Resident, Barangay Administrative Staff (Admin), Barangay Treasurer, Account Management Specialist, and Super Administrator.

2. Role: Resident Resident

Portal: Native Mobile (React Native / Expo) and Responsive Web Application
The Resident is the primary citizen client of the barangay who uses the mobile and web application to access public services, submit certification requests, track processing status in real time, and receive community advisories without waiting in long queues at the Barangay Hall.
Feature 2.1: Resident Registration and Identity Verification
Description: This feature enables residents residing within Barangay Zapatera to establish an authenticated digital identity by providing their personal demographics and uploading digital proof of residency.
Access Permissions: The resident can create a new registration record, input personal details including full name, birthdate, civil status, gender, specific sitio address, residency length, and voter status, and upload government-issued identification cards and proof of residency. The resident can view their own profile information and update personal contact details, civil status, and profile pictures.
Restrictions: The resident cannot create administrative or staff credentials, cannot view records of other residents, and cannot bypass identity verification.
Feature 2.2: Multi-Factor Authentication and Security Lockout Guard
Description: This feature protects resident accounts against brute-force intrusion by monitoring failed login attempts and locking accounts after three consecutive wrong password submissions.
Access Permissions: The resident can log into the system using their registered email and password, view active mobile or web session telemetry, and trigger an automated account unlock request to the barangay administration if their account becomes locked due to failed attempts.
Restrictions: The resident cannot unlock their own locked account without administrative intervention, cannot view security audit logs, and cannot bypass server-side rate limits.
Feature 2.3: Resident Operations Dashboard
Description: This feature serves as the resident's home screen, offering quick visual summaries of active applications, barangay emergency telephone hotlines, and published advisories.
Access Permissions: The resident can view personalized greeting information, track the real-time processing stage of ongoing requests, view instant hotline numbers for the Barangay Hall, Cebu City Police, and emergency medical responders, and access direct navigation shortcuts to document filing.
Restrictions: The resident cannot view global barangay statistics, other residents' applications, or internal administrative metrics.
Feature 2.4: Official Document Catalog and Requirements Checklist
Description: This feature provides transparent public information regarding all official certifications issued by Barangay Zapatera, including Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, Certificate of Residency, Business Clearance, Barangay ID, and etc.
Access Permissions: The resident can browse the complete catalog of official documents, view applicable statutory fees, examine estimated processing turnaround times, and review required supporting attachments prior to filing.
Restrictions: The resident cannot modify document guidelines, cannot alter official fees, and cannot delete document offerings from the catalog.
Feature 2.5: Online Document Application and Attachment Submission
Description: This feature guides the resident through a step-by-step digital application workflow to file formal certificate requests from any smartphone or computer.
Access Permissions: The resident can select a desired certificate, specify the exact legal purpose of request, input required application details, attach scanned images or photos of required documents such as CEDULA or valid IDs, select pickup preferences, review the application summary, and submit the request. Upon submission, the resident receives a unique tracking number formatted as BZ-YYYY-XXXX.
Restrictions: The resident cannot approve their own request, cannot generate or print official certifications, cannot alter assessed fees, and cannot edit submitted applications once administrative review has commenced.
Feature 2.6: Live Request Lifecycle Tracker and Resubmission Workflow
Description: This feature allows residents to monitor the progress of their submitted requests from initial pending submission to final document pickup, and permits correction of flawed submissions.
Access Permissions: The resident can view the real-time processing status across all stages including Pending, In Review, Approved, Ready for Pickup, and Claimed. If an application is marked as declined or flagged for resubmission due to invalid or blurry attachments, the resident can view specific administrative remarks, replace or upload corrected documents, and resubmit the application under the exact same tracking number without starting a new request.
Restrictions: The resident cannot view or track applications submitted by other residents, cannot alter administrative notes, and cannot delete requests that have already been approved or processed.
Feature 2.7: Barangay Public Bulletins, Announcements, and Citizen Engagement
Description: This feature keeps the community informed with verified advisories, disaster alerts, scheduled barangay programs, and public assemblies published by barangay officials.
Access Permissions: The resident can read published barangay news, view calendar schedules of community events, inspect pinned emergency bulletins, and post interactive comments or inquiries on published news threads.
Restrictions: The resident cannot publish, edit, pin, or delete official announcements, and cannot moderate or delete comments posted by other residents.
Feature 2.8: Real-Time Notification Center and Cellphone Push Alerts
Description: This feature notifies residents immediately whenever their application status progresses or when urgent community notices are posted.
Access Permissions: The resident can receive and view automated in-app notifications and browser or cellphone alerts regarding document approval, pickup readiness, fee payment instructions, or resubmission notices, and can mark notifications as read.
Restrictions: The resident cannot broadcast notifications or send unsolicited messages to other system users.
Feature 2.9: Resident Profile and Credential Management
Description: This feature allows residents to maintain their contact details and update account security settings.
Access Permissions: The resident can view and update their mobile phone number, emergency contact person, civil status, and profile avatar, and can securely change their account password.
Restrictions: The resident cannot alter their assigned role, cannot modify voter verification records, and cannot alter audit trail history.

3. Role: Barangay Administrative Staff / Admin Admin / Staff

Portal: Barangay Admin Operations Web Portal
The Barangay Administrative Staff member operates the administrative portal to evaluate resident document applications, inspect uploaded proofs, generate tamper-proof digital certificates, publish community notices, and assist residents with account lockouts.
Feature 3.1: Barangay Administrative Operations Dashboard
Description: This feature provides operational staff with an aggregated overview of daily barangay transactions, workload queues, and service metrics.
Access Permissions: The administrative staff member can view total application counts, pending review queues, approved certificates ready for release, declined request tallies, and upcoming scheduled community activities.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot modify global barangay institutional configurations, cannot change system branding, and cannot delete system audit records.
Feature 3.2: Document Application Processing and Evaluation Queue
Description: This feature serves as the central processing inbox where staff review incoming citizen applications and examine uploaded supporting requirements.
Access Permissions: The staff member can search and filter requests by tracking number, resident name, sitio, or status, view resident profiles, and inspect attached documents using the built-in File Preview Modal. The staff member can update application statuses to In Review, Approved, Declined, or Resubmission Requested, set estimated pickup dates, and append mandatory administrative remarks explaining approval requirements or rejection reasons. The staff member can also soft-delete fraudulent or duplicate requests into the archive with audit logging.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot approve requests without validating uploaded requirements, cannot waive statutory fees without treasurer authorization, and cannot record cash payments.
Feature 3.3: Approved Documents Registry and Claiming Management
Description: This feature organizes all approved certifications that have been cleared for physical release or claiming at the Barangay Hall.
Access Permissions: The staff member can view and filter approved applications awaiting pickup, verify the physical identity of the claiming resident, and update the status to Claimed upon handing over the certificate while recording the release timestamp and claiming officer identity.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot delete claimed records without generating a supervisory audit trail.
Feature 3.4: Automated Digital Certificate Generator and Printing Engine
Description: This feature dynamically creates standardized, legally compliant official certifications bearing the official seal of Barangay Zapatera, city logos, tracking codes, and signature blocks.
Access Permissions: The staff member can generate formatted digital certificates for Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, Certificate of Residency, Business Clearance, Barangay ID, and etc. The staff member can preview certificates complete with anti-fraud QR tracking codes and watermark seals, and can print or export the documents directly to PDF for official signing and issuance.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot alter the official barangay seal, modify official letterhead templates, or forge tracking numbers.
Feature 3.5: Official Document Guidelines and Requirements Management
Description: This feature allows administrative staff to maintain accurate public guidelines and requirements lists for each document type.
Access Permissions: The staff member can view and update document processing descriptions, required attachments checklists, processing turnaround times, and special instructions for applicants.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot alter core database schemas or delete statutory certificate offerings without SuperAdmin authorization.
Feature 3.6: Barangay Public Bulletins and Announcements Publishing
Description: This feature provides a content management tool for drafting and broadcasting official announcements to the resident portal.
Access Permissions: The staff member can create, view, edit, publish, pin, and delete barangay news, community advisories, disaster warnings, and event calendar dates, and can upload promotional banners.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot post unverified or unauthorized non-barangay notices.
Feature 3.7: Resident Account Security and Lockout Assistance
Description: This feature allows administrative staff to assist legitimate residents who have locked themselves out of their accounts due to three consecutive failed password attempts.
Access Permissions: The staff member can search resident profiles, verify resident identity, view account lock status, and execute an administrative unlock that resets the failed attempt counter to zero and restores resident portal access.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot view plaintext resident passwords, cannot change user roles, and cannot create or delete administrator accounts.
Feature 3.8: Archive and Soft-Delete Recovery Management
Description: This feature acts as an operational recycle bin to protect against accidental deletion of document records.
Access Permissions: The staff member can view soft-deleted document requests within the archive and restore erroneously deleted requests back to active processing queues.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot permanently destroy or purge database records, as permanent purging is strictly restricted to the Super Administrator.
Feature 3.9: Operational Processing Reports and Export
Description: This feature generates quantitative summaries regarding barangay document issuance volume, processing speed, and demographic demand.
Access Permissions: The staff member can filter operational statistics by custom date ranges and document categories, and can export structured reports into PDF, CSV spreadsheet, or printable summary formats.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot alter historical transaction numbers or access database server configurations.
Feature 3.10: Administrative Action History Logs
Description: This feature maintains an immutable audit trail of all actions executed by staff members to ensure complete operational transparency.
Access Permissions: The staff member can view a chronological log of administrative actions including request approvals, rejections, resubmission flags, certificate prints, and resident unlocks.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot edit, alter, or delete any log entry within the audit trail.
Feature 3.11: Staff Account Profile and Security Settings
Description: This feature allows administrative personnel to maintain their own system profile and login credentials.
Access Permissions: The staff member can view and update their personal profile information, contact number, and avatar, and can securely change their login password.
Restrictions: The staff member cannot elevate their own administrative privileges or reassign their own role.

4. Role: Barangay Treasurer Treasurer

Portal: Dedicated Barangay Treasurer Financial Web Portal
The Barangay Treasurer is the financial governance officer responsible for auditing document fees, logging cash collections, issuing Official Receipts (OR), recording fee waivers, and generating government accounting reports under strict Separation of Duties.
Feature 4.1: Financial Collection Dashboard and Real-Time Revenue Metrics
Description: This feature gives the Treasurer live financial intelligence regarding daily and monthly revenue performance across all document transactions.
Access Permissions: The Treasurer can view real-time calculations of Total Revenue Collected, Today's Cash Collections, Current Month Revenue, Unpaid/Pending Document Fees, and Fee-Exempt/Waived Transactions.
Restrictions: The Treasurer cannot evaluate document applications, approve certificates, or alter resident application guidelines.
Feature 4.2: Payment Collections and Official Receipt (OR) Registry
Description: This feature serves as the central cash register and financial ledger linked directly to all processed document requests.
Access Permissions: The Treasurer can view all document requests with assessed fee amounts, applicant names, document types, tracking numbers, and payment statuses. The Treasurer can update payment records by entering the official government Official Receipt (OR) Number, selecting the payment method such as Cash or GCash, inputting the exact amount paid, recording the payment date timestamp, and entering audit remarks. Furthermore, the Treasurer can officially authorize fee waivers for legally qualified residents under valid statutory grounds such as Indigency Certification, Senior Citizen status, PWD status, or the First-Time Jobseeker Act (Republic Act 11261).
Restrictions: Under database Row-Level Security policies, the Treasurer cannot insert or delete document requests, cannot issue digital certificates, and cannot alter application review statuses.
Feature 4.3: Revenue Breakdown Analytics by Document Type
Description: This feature displays graphical and proportional revenue breakdowns illustrating the financial contribution of each document classification.
Access Permissions: The Treasurer can view comparative income charts, collection volume percentages, and paid-versus-waived transaction ratios across all document classifications.
Restrictions: This is a read-only financial analytics module.
Feature 4.4: Official Financial Collection Reports and Audit Registers
Description: This feature generates formal accounting reports required for barangay financial auditing and reporting to higher government bodies.
Access Permissions: The Treasurer can filter financial records by date ranges, payment status, or receipt numbers, and can generate and export Daily Cash Collection Reports, Monthly Revenue Summaries, Official Receipt Registers, and Fee Exemption Ledgers in PDF, CSV spreadsheet, or formatted print layouts.
Restrictions: The Treasurer cannot falsify reconciled financial records or delete historical financial logs.
Feature 4.5: Treasurer Account Profile and Security Settings
Description: This feature enables the Treasurer to maintain personal profile details and security credentials.
Access Permissions: The Treasurer can view and update their profile details and change their secure login password.
Restrictions: The Treasurer cannot reassign their own role or grant financial recording authority to non-treasury personnel.

8. Key Defense Presentation Talking Points

1. Separation of Duties (SoD) for Financial Integrity
The architecture strictly separates document processing from fee collection. The Barangay Administrative Staff member can evaluate and approve documents, but is technically restricted from recording cash payments or issuing Official Receipts. Conversely, the Barangay Treasurer logs revenue and issues Official Receipts, but cannot approve document applications or issue certificates. This segregation of duties eliminates fraud, prevents unrecorded collections, and ensures audit accountability.
2. Multi-Layered Security and 3-Attempt Account Lockout Guard
To protect citizen and administrative accounts against automated brute-force password guessing and credential stuffing attacks, the system tracks consecutive failed attempts at both the application and database tiers. Upon the third consecutive failed attempt, the account is immediately locked, an unlock request record is dispatched, and access is rejected until an authorized administrator verifies the user's identity and performs an administrative unlock.
3. Transparent Resubmission Cycle and Tracking Continuity
Unlike traditional systems that outright reject incomplete applications, our platform introduces a dedicated Resubmission State. If an applicant uploads an expired ID or illegible proof, administrative staff provide clear deficiency instructions. The resident receives an immediate mobile alert, uploads the corrected attachment directly from their smartphone, and the application resumes processing under the original tracking number (BZ-YYYY-XXXX) without filing fees being forfeited.
4. Database-Level Row-Level Security (RLS) Enforcement
Access restrictions are not merely hidden in the frontend user interface; they are strictly enforced inside the Supabase PostgreSQL database via Row-Level Security policies. Even if an unauthorized user attempts to craft a direct API request to update document statuses or delete records, database policies automatically reject the transaction, guaranteeing data integrity and compliance with Republic Act 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
